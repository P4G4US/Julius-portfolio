About

S01

I'm Julius, a graphic designer in Bengaluru. I build brands people remember, from the first sketch of a mark to the last frame of motion.

I'm from Nagercoil, Tamil Nadu, and studied computer science at VIT. College is where design took over, leading graphic design for ADVITYA, our cultural fest. Today I help cafés, studios and builders show up online, and I like work people stop for, or watch twice.

      Process

      04030201020304

      Discovery

      Every project starts with a conversation. I learn who you are, who you are talking to and what the work has to do, then look at your space, your competitors and the culture around you before I start.

      Direction

      I turn what I learn into a clear direction: a moodboard, a type and colour palette, and two or three rough routes. We choose one together, so the look is agreed before any detail work starts.

      Design

      This is where it gets made. Logos, posters, social posts, emailers or motion pieces, built in Figma, Photoshop and After Effects. You see work in progress and shape it as we go, so there are no surprises at the end.

      Delivery

      I finish with files you can use straight away: print ready artwork, social formats, motion exports and, for identities, a guideline so your team can keep everything consistent long after handover.
